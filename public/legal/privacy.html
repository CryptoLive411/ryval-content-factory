Ryval Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This policy explains how Ryval collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Ryval app and Content Forge publishing tools.

1. Information we collect

Account and profile data you provide (name, email, sport, school, film, photos). If you connect TikTok, we store OAuth tokens and your TikTok display name so we can publish posts you authorize. We also collect basic usage and device logs needed to run the service.

2. How we use it

We use this information to operate Ryval, connect athletes and coaches, create and schedule content, publish to platforms you connect, keep the product secure, and communicate about the service.

3. Sharing

Athlete profiles may be visible to verified coaches. We share data with service providers that host the product (including cloud database and storage). If you connect TikTok, we send post images, captions, and account tokens to TikTok so the post can be published. We do not sell personal information.

4. Children

Ryval is used by youth athletes. Users under 18 need parental consent. Parents can review and request deletion of a linked athlete’s data.

5. Retention and rights

We keep data while your account is active. You may request access, correction, or deletion by emailing us. We delete account data within 30 days of a valid request, except where law requires retention.

6. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@ryval.app
